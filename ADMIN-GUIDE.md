# Editing the website (for Mom)

Sign in at **https://app.pagescms.org** with the GitHub account you were given, then pick the **chumaly** project.
Every change you save goes live on chumaly.com in about a minute.

## What you can change
- **Maltese Puppies / Yorkie Puppies** – add a puppy (name, description, photos), edit one, or delete one.
- **Gallery Photos** and **Pups Sold** – add or remove photos.
- **Reviews** – add a review (name, date, text), edit one, or delete one.

## Add photos
Open the section, click **Add an entry** on the photo list, then **upload** a photo from your phone or computer.
To remove one, delete its entry and press **Save**.

## Add a review
Open **Reviews** → **Add an entry** → type the name, date and the review → **Save**.

## Made a mistake?
Nothing is ever lost. Ask Dan to restore the earlier version.
