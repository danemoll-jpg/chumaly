# Editing the website (for Mom)

**Sign in:** go to **https://app.pagescms.org**, type your email, and click the link it emails you. Then open **chumaly**.
(Tip: bookmark it on your phone's home screen.)

On the left you'll see: **⭐ Reviews**, **📷 Gallery Photos**, **🏡 Happy Families**, **🐶 Maltese Puppies**, **🐶 Yorkie Puppies**.

## Add a review
1. Tap **⭐ Reviews**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Add an entry**.
3. Type the person's name and what they said.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Delete a review
Tap the **trash can** next to it, then tap **Save**.

## Add a picture with a caption
1. Tap **📷 Gallery Photos** (or **🏡 Happy Families**).
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Add an entry**.
3. Tap the photo box and **upload** a picture from your phone or computer.
4. Type a caption (or leave it blank).
5. Tap **Save**.

## Delete a picture
Tap the **trash can** next to it, then tap **Save**.

## Change the order
Press and drag the **⋮⋮ handle** on an item up or down. The top one shows first on the website.
The **top 3 reviews** also show on the home page. Tap **Save** when done.

## After you save
The website updates by itself in about a minute. Refresh the page to see it.

## Made a mistake?
Nothing is ever lost. Ask Dan to bring back the earlier version.
