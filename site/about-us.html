About Chumaly

If you are looking for your special baby face 100% Korean Maltese or Yorkshire Terrier puppy, you came to the right place. Chumaly is a small hobby breeder specializing in breeding quality top bloodlines from International Championship imports from S. Korea, Greece, Germany, Russia and Ukraine — healthy, quality, excellent temperament puppies. My much-loved Maltese and Yorkies weigh between 3 lbs. and 6 lbs. as adults. My babies are raised in my home and are puppy pad and tray trained. They come pre-spoiled and receive a lot of love, hugs and kisses. We are retired, so we have a lot of time to love them, and they love sitting in your lap.

Maltese are the sweetest, smartest and most beautiful breed.

Before my puppies go to their new home, they will be up to date with their vaccinations and worming. You will be provided with all the literature on how to take care of your new puppy, plus a contract, food, toy, outfit, etc. If you are interested in adding a new adorable loving Baby Maltese or Yorkie puppy, please contact me. My puppies come with a health guarantee contract and a one-year genetic guarantee. We breed for quality, not quantity, and I DNA and health test my breeders. It is very important that they are placed in exceptional homes. The majority of puppy buyers who adopt my babies neuter or spay them. New owners become my family for years, and most of my puppy buyers keep me posted on their new babies.

Thanks for visiting our site! Hope to hear from you. Please fill out a form to let me know which puppy you are interested in, or text me at 770-926-5340. My phone stays quite busy, so you can always text me or email me at chumaly@gmail.com and I will respond ASAP. References provided on request.

We have some beautiful baby face Maltese puppies available, also several retired adults at reasonable prices.

Thank you for your support and hope to hear from you.
