Our Maltese Parents

The Stars of Our Program

Behind every precious puppy are exceptional parents. These beautiful Korean baby doll dogs are the heart of our breeding program and our everyday companions. Our breeding dogs are the foundation of our program and are carefully selected for their outstanding health, beautiful structure, gentle temperaments, and breed quality. They spend their days playing, relaxing, and enjoying life as cherished members of our family.

Our breeders and retired babies are not for sale; they have a forever home with us. We simply love introducing the amazing dogs that make our puppies so special. We are honored to share them with you, as they represent the standard, dedication, and passion behind every puppy we raise.
